    <h3 class="news-title">PHOTOS INCLUDED IN ALMANSA SOCIAL PHOTOGRAPHY EXHIBITION</h3>

    <p>Three of my photographs have been selected for the Concurso de Fotografía Social Almansa 2026 exhibition video, organised by the Social Services Department of Almansa Town Council. The initiative forms part of the 40th anniversary of its Municipal Social Services. The competition drew 131 photographs from 52 participants in 25 different places in Spain and abroad, and the jury admitted 124 works.</p>

    <p>The collective exhibition brings together 52 photographs by 42 participants, and it shows social realities that so often go unnoticed in everyday life. It opened on 28 October at the Casa de Cultura de Almansa and can be visited until 27 November.</p>

    <p>The organisers have also made a video of the images submitted by all the participants. My three photographs appear between 3:06 and 3:17.</p>
