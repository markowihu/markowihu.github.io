2. Competition Awards & Gallery Exhibitions

  • Club Fotográfico Alicante (CFA) — 1st Prize, Liga Social Macro Category round - May-June 2026):
    • Awarded first place in the Macro category of one of six annual rounds in the Social League, during my first year as a member of the Club Fotográfico de Alicante.
    • Winning Entry: Carpobrotus acinaciformis: Geometría encendida (Glowing Geometry) — a detailed macro study capturing the radial symmetry and vivid magenta tones of an ice plant blossom in full bloom.
    • The Story Behind the Bloom: Captured along the sunny shores of Cartagena province, where these resilient succulents flower brilliantly under the Mediterranean sun.
    • El Palacio del Portalet Exhibition (Alicante): The fine-art print was selected for physical gallery display on the 4th floor of El Palacio del Portalet (Calle Labradores, 15, Alicante) as part of the collective annual CFA showcase, featuring approximately 18 selected works from member competition rounds and the June photo rally.
  • Exhibition Video Showcase:
    • A video walkthrough documenting the collective exhibition at El Palacio del Portalet in Alicante, showcasing the framed member prints on display.

3. Ongoing Projects, Focuses & Community Roles

  • Active Creative Series:
    • Found Minimalism: Ongoing study of urban and industrial geometry, structural order, and architectural detail.
    • Street & Humanity: Candid exploration of human presence, spatial balance, and street interactions in urban environments.
    • Sea & Sky / Natural World: Chromatic landscape series capturing Mediterranean coastal light, rugged mountain terrain, and horizon studies particularly in Alicante, Murcia and Almeria provinces.

  • International Group Administration & Community Curation:
    • Co-Administrator & Moderator for an active and popular international Flickr Group: active team member (total of three) managing group standards, photo pool curation and community discussions for a global photography group dedicated to high-vantage and perspective photography.

4. Distinction Pathways & Society Memberships

  • Member of Club Fotográfico Alicante (CFA), participating in competition leagues, collective gallery exhibitions and collaborative portfolio exchanges.
