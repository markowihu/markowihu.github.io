2. Competition Awards & Gallery Exhibitions

  • Club Fotográfico Alicante (CFA) — 1st Prize, Liga Social (Macro Category 2026):
    • Awarded First Place in the Macro category of the monthly Social League during my first year as a member of the Club Fotográfico de Alicante.
    • Winning Entry: Carpobrotus acinaciformis: Geometría encendida (Glowing Geometry) — a detailed macro study capturing the radial symmetry and vivid magenta tones of an ice plant blossom in full bloom.
    • The Story Behind the Bloom: Captured along the sunny coastal slopes of the Alicante province, where these resilient succulents flower brilliantly under the Mediterranean sun. When submitting the frame to competition coordinator Javier Trejo with botanical notes, Javier procedurally reminded me that league entry rules strictly accept photos only—no text or background stories allowed! This dedicated hub page provides the perfect space to share that context.
    • El Palacio del Portalet Exhibition (Alicante): The fine-art print was selected for physical gallery display on the 4th floor of El Palacio del Portalet (Calle Labradores, 15, Alicante) as part of the collective annual CFA showcase, featuring approximately 18 selected works from member competition rounds and the June photo rally.
