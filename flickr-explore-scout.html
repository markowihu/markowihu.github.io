Flickr Explore Scout

What is Flickr Explore?

Every day, Flickr’s automated Interestingness algorithm selects 500 photographs from hundreds of thousands of daily public uploads worldwide[1] to feature in Flickr Explore. Rather than relying on a single metric, the algorithm blends early organic engagement velocity (genuine views, favourites, and thoughtful comments) with metadata completeness, descriptive tagging, and community discovery. Because it continuously recalibrates, being featured follows an unpredictable rhythm, making every selection a welcome surprise.

Tracking Explored Work with Big Huge Labs Scout

The visual grid below is generated using Big Huge Labs Scout, a specialist utility that tracks every picture from a photographer’s stream selected for Explore, recording its peak daily ranking and feature date in reverse chronological order. Because the grid is a compact visual index, you can browse the full-resolution photographs, titles, and camera settings individually in my dedicated Flickr Explore Album, or inspect the live ranking history directly on the Big Huge Labs Scout tracker.
