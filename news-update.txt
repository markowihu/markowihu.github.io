Foto Walk 2026 — Alicante
Saturday, 26 September, 10:30 – 12:30 approx.

About the event:
A photo walk organised by the host of your local photography community. We meet, walk and take photos together — no likes or followers involved.

What to expect:
You'll spend around two hours exploring the city with other photographers from the Foto community. Along the way, you'll take photos, have your portrait taken, and chat with people who appreciate the same light as you.

This year's theme is contrast: light against shadow, old against new — anything that catches your eye and captivates you.

The walk finishes at a set point, sometimes followed by food or drink. No experience necessary. Bring whatever camera you have, including your phone.

The details:
Duration: ~2 hours
Bring: comfortable shoes, your camera, water and some money for food or drink.
Event cost: Free

Once you register, you'll receive a confirmation with the exact meeting point and any other information you need to know.

Photography is better in company, so we hope you'll join us.
