    <h3 class="news-title">LIGHT, CONTRAST AND MEMORY IN ALICANTE’S OLD QUARTER</h3>

    <p>Alicante’s <em>Casco Antiguo</em> (Old Quarter) possesses a distinct photogenic character that comes alive when low sunlight cuts diagonally across limestone walls, stone archways and historic façades. It is a compact urban fabric where centuries-old architecture sits just steps away from contemporary interventions, offering an ideal setting for both architectural geometry and candid street photography.</p>

    <p>That visual dialogue was the driving theme behind <strong><a href="https://markowihu.es/fotowalk-2026-en.html#fotowalk-2026-en">Foto Walk 2026</a></strong> in central Alicante, the international photo walk initiative by <em>Fotoapp.co</em> that I had the pleasure of co-organising and hosting on 26 September alongside my photography colleague Lev. Working around the theme of <em>Contrast</em>—light against shadow, old against new—we led an enthusiastic group of photographers through the streets and squares of the historic centre in a lively, collaborative morning session.</p>

    <p>Drawing on photographs from that walk as well as wider city work—more of which can be seen in my <a href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/markowihu/albums/72177720316895471/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alicante city gallery on Flickr</a>—I have selected two monochrome images for the <strong>XIX Otoño Fotográfico en el Casco Antiguo de Alicante</strong>, organised by the University of Alicante’s photography association (<em>Escritores de Luces</em>, E.L.U.A.) in collaboration with Alicante City Council and the Municipal Housing Board:</p>

    <ul>
        <li><strong>1. <em>Más allá del MACA</em> (Plaza de Santa María):</strong> A candid street scene outside Alicante’s Contemporary Art Museum contrasting the bold circular panels of the museum’s façade with the gestures of passers-by and visitors in the square.</li>
        <li><strong>2. <em>Fornix Urbis</em> (Plaza del Ayuntamiento, Alicante):</strong> An architectural chiaroscuro study in which a shadowed stone archway frames the bright open square and everyday movement beneath historic wrought-iron balconies.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>Registered as participant #51, both entries now await the jury’s decision. The winners and selected works will be announced on 17 December 2026, coinciding with the opening of the exhibition at Edificio Municipal El Claustro—after which I will share both photographs in the gallery.</p>
