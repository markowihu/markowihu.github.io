<strong>Foto Walk 2026 — Alicante</strong><br>
Saturday, 26 September, 10:30 – 12:30 approx.</p>

<p><strong>About the event:</strong><br>
A photo walk organised by the host of your local photography community. We meet, walk and take photos together — no likes or followers involved.</p>

<p><strong>What to expect:</strong><br>
You'll spend around two hours exploring the city with other photographers from the Foto community. Along the way, you'll take photos, have your picture taken (if you want), and chat with people who appreciate the same light as you.</p>

<p>This year's theme is contrast: light against shadow, old against new — anything that catches your eye and captivates you.</p>

<p>The walk finishes at a set point, sometimes followed by food or drink. No experience necessary. Bring whatever camera you have, including your phone.</p>

<p><strong>The details</strong><br>
Duration: ~2 hours<br>
Bring: comfortable shoes, your camera, water and some money for food or drink.<br>
Event cost: Free</p>
