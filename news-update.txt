    <h3 class="news-title">LIGHT AND CONTRAST IN ALICANTE’S OLD QUARTER</h3>

    <p>Alicante’s <em>Casco Antiguo</em> (Old Quarter) has a distinctive photogenic character that comes alive when low sunlight cuts diagonally across stone walls, imposing archways and historic façades. Its compact urban fabric features centuries-old buildings just steps away from contemporary interventions, offering an ideal setting for both architectural geometry and candid street photography.</p>

    <p>That visual dialogue was the driving theme behind <strong><a href="https://markowihu.es/fotowalk-2026-en.html#fotowalk-2026-en">Foto Walk 2026</a></strong> in central Alicante, the international photo walk initiative by <em>Fotoapp.co</em> that I had the pleasure of organising and co-hosting in September with my photography colleague Lev. Working around the theme of <em>Contrast</em> — light against shadow, old against new — we led an enthusiastic group of photographers through the streets and squares of the historic centre in a lively, collaborative morning session.</p>

    <p>Drawing on photographs from that walk as well as wider city work — more of which can be seen in my <a href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/markowihu/albums/72177720316895471/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alicante city gallery on Flickr</a> — I selected two monochrome images for the <strong>XIX Otoño Fotográfico en el Casco Antiguo de Alicante</strong>, organised by the University of Alicante’s photography association (<em>Escritores de Luces</em>, E.L.U.A.) in collaboration with Alicante City Council and the Municipal Housing Board:</p>
