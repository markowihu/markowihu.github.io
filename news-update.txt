    <h3 class="news-title">PHOTOS INCLUDED IN ALMANSA SOCIAL PHOTOGRAPHY EXHIBITION</h3>

	<p>Three of my photographs have been selected for the Concurso de Fotografía Social Almansa 2026 exhibition video, organised by the Social Services Department of Almansa Town Council. The initiative forms part of the 40th anniversary of its Municipal Social Services.</p>

	<p>The competition drew 131 photographs from 52 participants from 25 locations in Spain and abroad, with 124 works admitted by the jury. The collective exhibition brings together photographs by 42 participants, including mine. The gallery shows social realities that so often go unnoticed in everyday life, and is open from 28th October until 27th November at the Casa de Cultura in Almansa.</p>

	<p>In addition, the local council organisers have made a video of the selected images; my three photographs appear between 3m:06s and 3m:17s. I also enjoyed watching the variety of entries, and wouldn't want to miss offering congratulations to the winner!</p>
